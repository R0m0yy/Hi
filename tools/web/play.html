Paradise Untamed

Web build v0.3

Washed up on Haven Island

Gather, craft, fish and dive your way off the island. Nothing to install.

First start downloads about 20 MB.

Controls

W A S D
Move. Hold Shift to sprint, Space to jump.
E
Interact: gather, open, cook, board or leave a boat.
Left mouse
Use the item in your hand: chop, cast and reel, thrust the spear, eat, place.
Ctrl or C
Hold in deep water to dive, release to surface.
Tab or I
Pack and crafting.
1–6
Pick a hotbar slot. Z and X cycle.
Right mouse or Q
Cancel a cast or a placement.
R
Rotate while placing a structure.
Wheel
Zoom.
Esc
Pause menu: save, load, settings.

Good to know

  • Click the game once so it gets your keyboard.
  • The panel at the top left tells you what to do next.
  • A gamepad works too: left stick to move, A to jump, X to interact, RT to use.
  • Saves go to this browser. If the viewer blocks storage, saving will not stick.
  • This is the web version. It is lighter than the desktop build (no ambient occlusion), and the first frame can take a few seconds to compile shaders.
