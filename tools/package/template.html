How it plays

  • Fishing: hold use to charge the cast and release to throw. Press when the ! appears, then hold to reel and ease off when the tension runs high.
  • Spearfishing: hold Dive in deep water and press use to thrust. Underwater, the spear locks onto the nearest fish in front of you.
  • Boats: interact to board. W and S row, A and D steer, and interact again steps ashore.
  • Camp: sleeping in a shelter skips the night, heals, moves your respawn point and saves the game.
  • Settings: music, effects and ambience volume, fullscreen, interface size from 80 to 140%, tap-to-dive instead of hold, and objectives on or off.

Research

Where the references come from

Phase 0 tried to find the original assets behind both references and whether they can be licensed. No candidate could be confirmed, because image search and the asset marketplaces are blocked from this environment. Every classification rests on text sources.

Research limits

Reverse image search (Google Lens, TinEye, Bing) was unavailable. Superhive, itch.io, Kenney, Quaternius, Poly Pizza, Sketchfab, BlenderKit, ambientCG, ArtStation and YouTube pages were refused by the network proxy. Server-side text search worked and supplied every finding below. Both reference files carry the same 138-byte EXIF block with no creator, software or copyright tags.

Art bible

A sunlit, handcrafted miniature world

Islands read as sculpted dioramas, as in Ref A: soft sand slabs, chunky bevelled rock stacks, exaggerated curvy palms, props tied from driftwood and rope. Framing, colour temperature and water clarity come from Ref B: golden sand, crystal turquoise shallows, a cream foam line, deep blue past the reef, and a small readable hero.

Target albedo palette

Water colour by depth below the surface

Drawn to scale from 0 to 16 m. Water stays clear rather than milky, so the real seabed shows through down to about 3 m.

    Lighting

    Sun
    Warm #FFE7C2, energy about 1.75, about 48° high, from the east (screen right), so camera-facing walls are lit
    Shadows
    Soft PCF, shadow opacity 0.72, so shaded sand stays warm and readable
    Ambient
    Sky plus #B8B0EB at 0.95. Shade reads lavender, never grey or black.
    Occlusion
    SSAO, radius about 1.5 m, plus ambient occlusion baked into rock vertex colours
    Tonemap, glow
    Filmic tonemap. Glow only on foam, sun glints and fire.

    Camera

    Projection
    Narrow perspective, FOV 38°, measured from Ref B and chosen over three other candidates in an A/B test. Orthographic is one setting away.
    Angle
    Pitch −40°, yaw 18°, so props face the camera almost square-on
    Zoom
    6 to 110 m of view at the focus; 11 m by default since v0.3 (17 m in v0.2). Eases in a little while diving.
    Tracking
    Critically damped follow with a small look-ahead. Never rotates by itself.

    Colours sampled from the references

    K-means clusters for each object region, from tools/palette/sample_palette.py. Bar widths are each cluster's share of the region. The hex value is the largest cluster.

    Screenshot acceptance checklist

    • Sand reads golden in sun and lavender in shadow.
    • The seabed shows through the shallows, with a clear turquoise-to-blue gradient.
    • A crisp, animated cream foam line follows the shore.
    • Palms are curved and chunky, in recognisable variants, with shadows on the sand.
    • Rocks are rounded, bevelled and lavender-grey, with grass caps where flat.
    • Props look handcrafted, with lashings and irregularity.
    • The hero is readable at the default zoom.
    • Nothing looks like a placeholder primitive.

    Not allowed

    • Pixel art, sprites, billboarded scenery or painted backgrounds.
    • Photoreal scanned textures, realistic humans, military gear.
    • Mixed packs that break the palette or the facet rules.
    • Pure white foam, pure black shadows, grey untextured materials.
    • Primitive boxes and spheres left as final art.
    • AI-generated images used as assets or shown as gameplay.

    Build

    How the game is made and checked

    Every model and every sound is generated by a script, so any asset can be rebuilt from source. Model rebuilds are byte-identical, because face order is canonicalised before export.

    1. Generate the assets in Blender

      Python generators in tools/blender/ build the islands, palms, rocks, props, foliage, items, fish and the explorer, bake ambient occlusion into vertex colours and export GLB files. Material names choose the Godot shader.

      tools/build_all.sh                    # every group, then import and scenes
tools/build_all.sh explorer items     # palms rocks props foliage terrain character items explorer islands

    2. Synthesise the sound

      47 effects and 6 loops from numpy: plucked strings, filtered noise and envelopes. The loops are encoded to Ogg Vorbis with ffmpeg.

      /opt/bpyenv/bin/python tools/audio/make_audio.py

    3. Import into Godot and assemble the islands

      A headless import, then tools/build_haven_scene.gd writes the Haven Island, Palm Key and Lookout Isle scenes from their layouts. At load time, WorldPopulator turns the set dressing into gameplay: palms and rocks become resources, driftwood and shells become pickups, and the camp gets a working fire, chest and boat.

    4. Test

      World checks, the controller test, and the end-to-end gameplay test with simulated input.

      godot --headless --path game --script res://tools/smoke_test.gd
godot --headless --path game --script res://tools/player_test.gd
godot --headless --path game --script res://tools/gameplay_test.gd

    5. Capture and record

      --fixed-fps 30 keeps game time at 1/30 s per frame even when rendering is slow, so timed moments such as a bite or a cooking bar are not skipped. Movie Maker mode renders every frame of the scripted run for the video.

      godot --path game --fixed-fps 30 --script res://tools/capture_gameplay.gd -- --out=/abs/dir
godot --path game --write-movie /abs/demo.avi --fixed-fps 30 --resolution 1280x720 --script res://tools/demo_video.gd
godot --path game --script res://tools/perf_probe.gd

    6. Export the desktop builds

      Linux, Windows and macOS presets, built with the official Godot 4.4.1 export templates (SHA-512 checked against the release). The script copies the Godot licence, the licence manifest and a play guide next to each build.

      tools/export_desktop.sh               # Linux Windows macOS
tools/export_desktop.sh Linux

    7. Package this page

      python3 tools/package/build_package.py

    Toolchain

    Godot
    4.4.1 stable. The editor is the Ubuntu archive build; the desktop builds use the official 4.4.1 export templates.
    Blender
    5.2.2 as the bpy Python module (PyPI wheel, Python 3.13)
    Audio
    numpy for synthesis, ffmpeg with libvorbis for the loops
    Renderer
    Vulkan through Mesa llvmpipe under Xvfb. There is no GPU here, so frames take about a second each, but they are genuine engine renders.
    On your machine
    Run a desktop build, or install Godot 4.4 or newer, open game/project.godot and press Play.

    v0.2.0

    Environment refinement

    • Camera: narrow perspective (FOV 38°, pitch −40°, yaw 18°) measured from Ref B and A/B-tested; orthographic still available; zoom out to 110 m for whole-island views.
    • Rock stack: rebuilt with chamfered lavender-grey slabs, bevel rims, pits, inlays and a basin; baked vertex AO; stone mottling material; a deeper cave with a boulder arch and dark floor; grass caps with planted tufts; Ref A-style vegetation around the base.
    • Palms: trunks lean out and recover toward vertical; 7 variants plus twin and trio; two-layer crowns; richer greens.
    • Set dressing: designed vignettes (groves, shrub, flower and broadleaf corners, dune grass, coastal rocks, driftwood, tide-line shells) with new bushes, agaves, banana plants, flowering shrubs, beach grass, driftwood, shells, coconuts and rock clusters.
    • Terrain: fixed a bug that left the interior with no vertices; real dunes, a camp-to-cave trail, grass in patches.
    • Sand and lighting: ripples, mottling and sparkles; sun moved to the east; shadow opacity 0.72; warmer ambient.
    • Water: kept as approved. Glints fade at distance, and a deep floor plane hides the terrain edge.
    • Castaway: original character with a 17-bone rig and 11 animations; PlayerController passes 14 of 14 checks.
    • Reproducible builds: every asset is now byte-identical across rebuilds.
