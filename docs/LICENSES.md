# Licence manifest

Everything shipped in `game/` and in the desktop builds is listed here. Per-file
detail (origin, generator script, approval status) is in `ASSET_MANIFEST.csv`.

No asset was bought, and nothing from a paid or restricted source is included.
`CHARACTER_ASSET_RESEARCH.md` records the paid character options that were reviewed
and not downloaded.

## Original project work

Everything else was made for this project and belongs to it.

- **3D models:** islands, palms, rocks, props, foliage, fish, items, tools and the
  explorer's mesh. They are generated by the Blender Python scripts in `tools/blender/`.
  The explorer's mesh, clothing and skin weights are original. Only the rig and
  animation clips come from Quaternius (CC0).
- **Shaders:** water, terrain, foliage, character and fish, in `game/shaders/`.
- **Sound effects and music loops:** synthesised by `tools/audio/make_audio.py`
  (numpy: Karplus-Strong plucks, band-limited noise, envelopes). No samples were recorded
  or downloaded.
- **UI icons:** item icons are rendered from the item models by
  `game/tools/bake_icons.gd`. Stat icons are drawn by `tools/ui/make_ui_icons.py`.
- **Code:** all GDScript and Python in this repository.
